## Capture and comparison protocol

The reference PNGs are synthetic, variable-height full-content exports, 780 px wide and intended for a 390 logical-pixel phone width. Normalize comparison width only, preserve each image's aspect ratio and full-content height, and compare matching app viewport and scroll states. Do not distort image height or treat raw pixel equality as a quality claim. Report the viewport, scroll state, theme/text scale, and harness used for each capture.

Use synthetic production-widget data only. Do not hardcode Stitch demo names, IDs, dates, cash amounts, addresses, statuses, or other metadata into the app. Keep expected screenshot assets free of credentials and private customer evidence.

All after images link to byte-for-byte copies under implementation/screenshots/after; [capture provenance](implementation/CAPTURE-PROVENANCE.md) records source paths, physical dimensions, SHA-256, and known capture settings. Before (B) images remain local, ignored build outputs and are not portable. Operations captures use 390×884 at DPR 2; delivery uses 390×947 with multiple scroll states retaining destination, contact, and instructions. Auxiliary captures use real SDK fonts where identified. The responsive Home/history/OTP captures are the latest available visual outputs. The Home screenshot fixture has generatedAt=null; a separate final widget check verified populated timestamps and View all actions.

The earlier invalid auxiliary OTP-success and Ahem-font policy baselines remain preserved under build/stitch-ui/task-003-invalid-baselines-20261112.
